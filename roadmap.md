# Future functionalities

The idea is to keep **updating** the project:

* Compare **more than 2** drivers simultaneously
* Add **laptime info**

  * **Laptime** for each lap in the slider
  * **Tyre colored** lap in the slider
  * **Sector** times
  * **Mark** sectors in the graphs
  * Show **turn sections**
* **Delta** graph
* Track **colored by telemetry**
* **Weather** conditions
* Graphs over time

# Future integrations

There's also the idea of ​​expanding this project to **other fields**:

* Load **simulator** telemetry, to compare it to real F1 telemetry (Assetto Corsa, rFactor2, Iracing...)

  * It will bring more tools (individually analize the sim telemetry with more channels)
* **Real-time** F1 telemetry

\*Each of these integrations will be developed in a separate branch
